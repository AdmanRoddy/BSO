BENT STEERING OFFROAD - SAMSUNG TV APP
======================================

Your own copy of the Bent Steering app with the home screen, BSO TV, J R-dio and your
blueprints built in. It installs through Samsung's Developer Mode and does not change the
TV's software. To remove it, uninstall it like any other app.

Keep this folder to yourself. The blueprints inside are private.


1. TURN ON DEVELOPER MODE ON THE TV (one time)
   a. On this computer, double-click "Install Bent Steering.bat". It shows this computer's
      address at the top (for example 192.168.1.23). Leave the window open.
   b. On the TV, press Home and go to Apps.
   c. On the Apps screen, press 1 2 3 4 5 on the remote. On a remote without number keys,
      press the 123 button and use the number pad on the screen.
   d. In the Developer Mode box, switch Developer mode ON, type this computer's address
      into Host PC IP, and press OK.
   e. Restart the TV fully: hold the remote's power button until the Samsung logo shows,
      or unplug the TV for 30 seconds.

2. FIND THE TV'S ADDRESS
   On the TV: Settings > General > Network > Network Status > IP Settings.

3. INSTALL
   In the installer window, type the TV's address and press Enter. It connects, sends the
   app, installs it and opens it on the TV. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC",
   click More info, then Run anyway.

4. MAKE IT START WITH THE TV
   On the TV: Settings > General > Smart Features.
     Autorun Last App: ON
     Autorun Smart Hub: OFF
   The TV reopens the last app you used, so finish on Bent Steering before turning the TV
   off. Back out of Netflix or YouTube, and Bent Steering is there.

UPDATES
   BSO TV, J R-dio and the home screen update by themselves from the website. When the
   home screen shows "Update", run the newest installer the same way.

ON THE REMOTE
   Arrows and OK      move and open
   Back               close, or go back to the home screen
   Channel up/down    change BSO TV channels; next station on J R-dio
   Play/Pause         pause and play
   Red button         captions on BSO TV; categories on J R-dio
